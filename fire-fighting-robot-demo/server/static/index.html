PDF AUTOPILOT PRIORITY

Sensor decision logic

ADC
  1. Right <250STOP · pump ON · servo 90°→40°→90°
  2. Front <350STOP · pump ON · servo sweeps 90°–140°–40°–90°
  3. Left <250STOP · pump ON · servo 90°→140°→90°
  4. Right 251–700Pump OFF · reverse 100 ms · turn right 200 ms
  5. Front 350–800Pump OFF · forward
  6. Left 251–700Pump OFF · reverse 100 ms · turn left 200 ms
  7. OtherwiseSTOP · pump OFF

PDF branches are evaluated in this order; setup sweeps 90°→140°→40°→95° in 5° steps. Fire depletion after 4 s of virtual pumping is a room-simulation effect, not PDF firmware.
